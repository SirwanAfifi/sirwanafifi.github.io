# About Sirwan Afifi

Sirwan Afifi is a seasoned software engineer and technical lead with extensive experience developing web applications in the UK's high-tech sector. He specialises in the Node.js ecosystem, React, and TypeScript, and has worked with Deno, Next.js, Gatsby, GraphQL, Nest.js, ASP.NET Core, and C#.

His work includes leading technical teams, designing scalable and maintainable systems, cloud architecture across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, SQL and NoSQL databases, and DevOps automation with PowerShell and Bash.

Sirwan contributes to open-source projects and writes regularly about emerging technologies and practical engineering lessons.
