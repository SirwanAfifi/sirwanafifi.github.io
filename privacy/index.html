Privacy notice_

This is a personal publishing site. It has no visitor accounts, checkout, advertising profiles, or contact forms. This notice describes the limited information that may be processed when you browse the site or contact me.

Last updated 22 August 2026

Hosting and delivery

Pages are published through GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. Like most web infrastructure, those providers may process IP addresses, request URLs, browser or user-agent information, timestamps, and security events in operational logs. They use that information to deliver content, maintain reliability, prevent abuse, and diagnose faults under their own privacy terms.

Preferences and third-party content

The theme switcher stores one value named agent-theme in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and is used only to remember the selected visual theme. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser may make requests to Google. Blog post comments are provided by giscus and GitHub; the comments component is loaded from giscus and interaction with it is governed by those services. Ordinary outbound links may also take you to sites with their own policies.

Email and retention

If you email me, your address, message, and any attachments are processed by the mail provider and retained in the mailbox for as long as they are useful for the conversation, record keeping, or security. Please do not send secrets or sensitive personal data. You may ask me to delete an ordinary correspondence thread when there is no legal or security reason to retain it.

Questions

For a privacy question or deletion request, use the details on thecontact page. Please identify the relevant message or interaction so I can find it without requesting additional personal information.
