## How Sirwan trains

The current goal is building muscle through a pragmatic, hypertrophy-oriented three-session rotation. Most work is in the 8–12 rep range, using a mix of machines, cables, barbells, and dumbbells.

### Chest + Arms

Bench or incline pressing, a fly variation, then direct biceps work. Typical duration: ≈ 60–90 min.

### Shoulders + Triceps

Shoulder pressing, lateral and rear-delt work, followed by triceps. Typical duration: ≈ 50–60 min.

### Legs + Lats

Pulldowns and rows alongside leg press, extension, curl, and adduction work. Typical duration: ≈ 40–55 min.
