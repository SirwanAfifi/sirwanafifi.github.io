# Privacy Notice

Last updated: 22 August 2026

This is a personal publishing site. It has no visitor accounts, checkout, advertising profiles, or contact forms. This notice describes the limited information that may be processed when you browse the site or contact Sirwan Afifi.

## Hosting and delivery

Pages are published through GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. Like most web infrastructure, those providers may process IP addresses, request URLs, browser or user-agent information, timestamps, and security events in operational logs. They use that information to deliver content, maintain reliability, prevent abuse, and diagnose faults under their own privacy terms.

## Preferences and third-party content

The theme switcher stores one value named `agent-theme` in the browser's local storage. It stays on the visitor's device and is used only to remember the selected visual theme.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so the browser may make requests to Google. Blog post comments are provided by giscus and GitHub; interaction with that component is governed by those services. Ordinary outbound links may lead to sites with their own policies.

## Email and retention

If you email Sirwan, your address, message, and any attachments are processed by the mail provider and retained for as long as they are useful for the conversation, record keeping, or security. Do not send secrets or sensitive personal data. You may ask for an ordinary correspondence thread to be deleted when there is no legal or security reason to retain it.

## Questions

For a privacy question or deletion request, use the [contact page](https://sirwan.info/contact.md). Identify the relevant message or interaction so it can be found without requesting additional personal information.
