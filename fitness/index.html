01

Where I am now

My current goal is building muscle. The shape of the training is hypertrophy-oriented, structured, and pragmatic: most work sits around 8–12 reps, with a mix of machines, cables, barbells, and dumbbells. I use selected failure sets and some supersets; I do not take every set to failure.

The current structure is a three-session rotation rather than a promise to train on fixed days. That distinction matters. The routine gives me something stable to return to, while real life decides exactly how the week looks.

Read this first

This is a personal training log, not medical advice, coaching, or a programme for someone else to copy. Results, tolerances, and health circumstances differ.
